Established • Skill Development • Career Training

A learning centre focused on practical skills, workplace capability and career readiness.

CITI Academy is an organisation established with a clear purpose: to provide practical skill development and professional training that helps learners build confidence, workplace capability and career-ready knowledge.

For more than 23 years, CITI Academy has been serving learners through structured training, practical sessions and trainer guidance. Over 10,000 students have received training through the institute, and more than 5,000 students have received placement opportunities through its training and career-support ecosystem.

Our learning approach brings together classroom concepts, hands-on practice, projects, assessments, interview preparation and professional guidance — helping learners turn knowledge into useful skills.
